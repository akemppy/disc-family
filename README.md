# Family DISC

A static, phone-first family comparison of DISC reports on two sliders: pace (fast vs slow) and priority (the people in the room vs the work). Open anyone, or pick two people and read the gap, the leftover blend, and how it shows up at home.

First names only. No emails. No last names. Not a clinical instrument, not for hiring.

## Open locally

No build step. From this folder:

- Double-click `index.html`, or
- `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open http://localhost:8080/

Hash routes (shareable):

- `#/` everyone, family 2x2 teaser
- `#/p/alex` (and derek, ashley, renee, mike, elliana, sofia, colin, kate)
- `#/vs/alex/derek` any pair
- `#/family` the house on pace × people vs the work

Full original reports open on https://disc-profile.netlify.app from each person page.
