# Family DISC

A static, phone-first comparison of one family's DISC reports. Open anyone's page, or pick two people and read both directions: what each will admire, what will grind, how to talk, how they fight, and what to do this week.

First names only. No emails. No last names. Not a clinical instrument, not for hiring.

## Open locally

No build step. From this folder:

- Double-click `index.html`, or
- `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open http://localhost:8080/

Hash routes (shareable):

- `#/` everyone, family map, all-vs-all matrix
- `#/p/alex` (and derek, ashley, renee, mike, elliana, sofia, colin, kate)
- `#/vs/alex/derek` any pair, both directions
- `#/family` shared profiles, clusters, who to send into a hard conversation

Full original reports open on https://disc-profile.netlify.app from each person and pair page.
